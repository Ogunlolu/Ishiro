# Ishiro — brand direction (draft v1)

Status: proposed 18 Nov 2026, not yet approved. Visual board and sample
screens: https://claude.ai/artifact/BS92wjiJh4MF1Gyx1mzChF (private to the owner).

## Who it is for

Friends aged 18–38 on group trips. Launch markets: North America and the UK.

## Promise

**Split the trip. Keep the friends.**

Everyone sees the same numbers, the person who paid gets thanked, and the trip
ends square. Trust is the foundation; fun is the reason people choose it.

## What we take from apps that worked

These are my readings of well-known products, not cited research.

| From | What worked | What Ishiro takes |
|---|---|---|
| Strava | Kudos, a feed of activities, shareable activity cards, one colour owned completely | Thanks for the payer; the trip as a feed; one unmistakable brand colour |
| Spotify Wrapped | Year-end recap as bold, screenshot-ready cards with playful labels | Trip Wrapped with awards, built to share in the group chat |
| Duolingo | A distinct voice and small celebrations | Celebrate settling up. We do not copy the guilt-trip reminders |
| Monzo (UK) | Plain human language about money; a colour nobody else had | Plain words, never bank language; an ownable colour |
| Cash App, Venmo (North America) | Amount-first entry; payments as social moments | Big-number keypad first; expenses read as "Ada got dinner" |
| Partiful | Link-first, no-download-needed group organising that feels like a party | Join a trip from a link, no account |
| Splitwise, Tricount, Splid | Fewest-payments settling, join by link, offline | All of it, as the functional base |

What we deliberately avoid: red/green debt colouring, daily limits and ads
(Splitwise's weak spot), shaming whoever owes, and leaderboards of debt.

## Personality and voice

The fun friend who is good with money: warm, witty but never petty, fair to
everyone. Celebrates the payer; never shames the person who owes.

- "Ada got dinner. Say thanks?" not "New expense added by Ada."
- "Ben, you're up. You've fronted the least." not "Ben has an outstanding debt."
- "All square. Trip closed clean." not "All balances have been settled."

Rules: short sentences; the person's name first; "gets back" and "owes", never
"debt" or "debtor"; jokes point at the trip, never at someone's balance. Spelling
and currency follow the user's locale (US, Canada, UK). Each trip has a tone
setting, Playful (default) or Plain, for groups where jokes about money would
land badly.

## Visual identity

- **Wordmark:** "ishiro", lowercase, in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold.
- **Signature shape:** passport-style stamps. Awards, thank-yous and the
  "Closed clean" mark on a settled trip all arrive as stamps.
- **Colour**

  | Name | Hex | Use |
  |---|---|---|
  | Mango | `#FFB627` | Brand colour, main buttons (always with Ink text) |
  | Ink | `#1B1530` | Text, dark surfaces |
  | Paper | `#FFFFFF` | Cards |
  | Mist | `#F5F3FA` | App background |
  | Lagoon | `#0B7A75` | "Gets back", done states |
  | Violet | `#6D3BD0` | "Owes" |
  | Coral / Sky / Mint / Lilac | `#FF8A7A` `#7CC8FF` `#8EE3B5` `#C9B8FF` | People's avatars, award stickers (with Ink text) |

  Owing is never red. A balance always carries words as well as colour.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, amounts and stamps; each
  phone's system font for everything else.
- **People:** every member gets a sticker colour and an initial, so "who" is
  recognisable at a glance.
- **Motion and touch:** quick (150–300 ms); a stamp "thunk" with a light
  vibration when a payment is marked done; a bigger moment when the trip
  closes clean. Honours reduced-motion settings.
- **Accessibility:** text contrast at least 4.5:1, touch targets at least
  44 pt, works with large system text, no meaning carried by colour alone.

## Signature moments

1. **Thanks for the payer** — every expense reads "Ada got dinner" with a
   one-tap thanks.
2. **Whose turn** — "Ben, you're up": a gentle prompt that keeps things even.
3. **Square up** — few payments, a "Why $90?" explanation on each, a progress
   bar, and the Closed clean stamp when everyone is even.
4. **Trip Wrapped** — shareable recap with awards (The Banker, Uber Royalty,
   Minister of Snacks, Fastest to square up).
